\begin{itemize}
\item A new wide-angle propagation method from scalar optics is introduced to electron microscopy.
\item Wide-angle corrections to electron multislice propagation are systematically compared.
\item Exact angular-spectrum propagation removes most Fresnel error at no extra per-slice cost.
\item Local-medium propagation gives a smaller correction at substantially higher cost.
\item Wide-angle errors shift diffraction peaks and alter diffraction amplitudes.
\end{itemize}
