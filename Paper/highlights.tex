\begin{itemize}
\item Wide-angle multislice propagators are benchmarked for electron microscopy.
\item AS-MS corrects most Fresnel wide-angle error at near-identical cost.
\item WP-MS adapts scalar-optics wave propagation to electron multislice.
\item WP-MS is closest to the ODE reference under the tested conditions.
\item Practical regimes are identified for F-MS, AS-MS, and WP-MS.
\end{itemize}
