# Ultramicroscopy submission readiness

Reviewed against the Ultramicroscopy Guide for Authors on 16 July 2026:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/ultramicroscopy/publish/guide-for-authors

## Manuscript status

- Article type: Original research Article.
- Scope: Electron microscopy, method development, imaging and scattering
  theory, and computational methods.
- Source format: Editable LaTeX using the Elsevier `elsarticle` class.
- Title page: Title, author names, full affiliation, author email addresses,
  and corresponding-author designation are present.
- Abstract: 181 words; the journal limit is 250 words.
- Keywords: Six; the journal allows one to seven.
- Highlights: Five bullets in a separate editable file; every bullet is at
  most 85 characters, including spaces.
- Structure: Numbered sections and subsections, editable equations, an
  editable table, a glossary, figure captions, a data-availability statement,
  an AIDAS funding statement, a CRediT statement, and the required
  generative-AI declaration are present.
- References: Numeric citation style is used; all cited keys resolve, and the
  bibliography includes DOI links where available.
- Figures: Figures are supplied separately as `Figure_1.pdf` through
  `Figure_7.pdf`, matching their first-appearance order in the manuscript.
  Every figure is cited in the text and has a caption; multipart captions
  describe each lettered panel in sequence. The PDFs have embedded fonts.
  Figure-generation sources now request 600 dpi for mixed raster/vector
  artwork, exceeding the journal's 500 dpi recommendation for combination
  artwork.
